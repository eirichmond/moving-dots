Burger Menu Demo

Click the icon in the top-right corner to see the burger animate into an X as the menu opens, then back to three lines when it closes.

The top and bottom lines slide to the centre and rotate ±45°. The middle line fades out simultaneously. All transitions are CSS-driven using cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1).
